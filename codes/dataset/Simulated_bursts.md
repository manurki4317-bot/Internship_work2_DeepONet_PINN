## Access to Generated Simulation Datasets

During the generation and preprocessing of the simulated neuronal bursts, several datasets and intermediate files are produced (raw simulations, processed datasets, training/testing splits, figures, and auxiliary files).

Since including all these files directly in the repository is impractical due to their size, they have been uploaded to a shared Google Drive folder.

You can access and download all the required files using the following shared link:

**Google Drive folder:**  
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1rmggacGoSs5qNj8ho2j4dcPOmbkJRiFY

This folder contains the simulated datasets and supporting files required to reproduce the experiments, train the implemented models, and perform the corresponding analyses.
